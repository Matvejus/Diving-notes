


## Before the course starts
Prepare all the equipment
- Get the twinset tanks, 70 bars in the tanks, make sure that the tanks are in good condition
- Get the backplate - make sure it is in a good condition
- Get the regs - back gas/ deco reg. Check all the hoses, check spgs, test for bubbles and leaks
- Order deco gas - 50% mix
- Prepare the rigging for the deco gas - make sure you have enough of everything
- Prepare a drop tank - 80%

## Day 1


Welcome, show the dive center, explain where to find what and where to store equipment. Explain the compressor room and how to get gas.

Open the bar bill for the customer

Do the paperwork - TDI booklet, liability release, medical form
ask about the experience

Advanced nitrox theory in one day.

When they are doing the exam, get the gear ready

### Explain the twinset
- Tanks - weight and material
- Tank bands - what are they for, space between bolts
- Manifold - how the gas moves, what does the manifold do - which side to rotate to open and close
- Valves on each tank - explain that each valve will have it's own reg
- Explain the regs and what goes on which side: Short-hose (what is short hose), spg; Longhose (what is longhose and how it works), lpi. Why only one lpi. Explain the orientation.
- Explain the wing, different types of wings. Doughnut, Horseshoe. Bungees. Lift capacity. Dual single bladder. 
- Explain the backplate - different materials - why? One size fits all. One continious loop.
- Start sizing up the backplate (one by one) - follow the guide. 
- Assemble the twinset yourself - slowly, step by step.
- Explain how the wing can be placed, higher or lower to adjust the buoyancy - which has slots that match the space of twinset
- explain how the backplate can be placed higher or lower - what willl that affect
- explain how to attach regs - short hose first, then longhose. Angle and routing
- Analayze the contents of the tank, explain how to analayze, label, log - team analysis
- Test the rig - valves, pressure, regs, inflate deflate, dump gas. 
- Show how to set up the deco. Analyze, label, log.
- Explain the drop tank and it's purpose


## Day 2

### Pool session - Bring a camera
Get the gear in the pool, get the tanks on the edge, so they can be taken later.

Do the start drill (Explain what is a start drill and why is it important and helpful)

Explain the skills that we will be doing.

#### No deco
- swimming around, buoyancy
- kicking
	- Frog kicks - make them swim around the pool (clock wise, anti clock wise), explain the they should turn using helicopter turns
	- Modified frog kicks using only ankles
	- Modified flutter kicks
	- Helicopter kicks - make each of them swim to the middle of the pool and rotate in different directions.
	- Back kicks - use modified frog to get to the other side of the pool and use back kicks to get back
- OOG - victim and donor - explain all the steps
- Valve shutdowns - steps, remind to open after the skill is done
	- Short hose
	- long hose - switch regs, abort dive
	- manifold
- Valve drill (bubbles overhead) - step by step. Close the manifold (isolate the tanks, so you only loose the gas from one tank), Check the spg (now the tanks are isolated, if the needle is falling it means the right valve is broken, if the needle stays - right tank). Remind to open the manifold right after the tanks are closed. And open the valve after cutting the skill
	- short hose
	- long hose
