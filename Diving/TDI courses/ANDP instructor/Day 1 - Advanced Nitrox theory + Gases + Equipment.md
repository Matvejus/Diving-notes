#### Twinset bolt spacing 11 inch or 28 cm: 
In 1984, when the diving equipment company began commercially manufacturing the first aluminum backplates, there was no baseline standard for sizing. The company's co-founder, **Lamar Hires**, sent a prototype design to a machine shop to have the mounting holes drilled. The raw aluminum plate was exactly **15 inches long** from top to bottom. When the machinist asked where to place the holes, Hires simply instructed him to drill them **2 inches from the top and 2 inches from the bottom**. Subtracting those 4 inches from the 15-inch plate left exactly **11 inches center-to-center** between the holes
